Methodology

A disciplined approach built around stability, clarity, and respect for real-world constraints.

01

Understand the environment

We start by understanding where the system will live: hardware, users, connectivity, and operational limits.

02

Design for locality

Models, data, and interfaces are selected and configured to function locally — cloud access is optional, never required.

03

Document and constrain

Every system includes clear documentation, usage boundaries, and predictable failure modes.

04

Deliver for longevity

We favor boring, understandable solutions that continue working months and years later.
